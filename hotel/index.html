Welcome to The Grand Azure

Space to pause. Room to reconnect.

Set along a beautiful stretch of coastline, The Grand Azure is imagined as a place to step away from the everyday and settle into a more considered rhythm. Natural textures, open spaces, and warm, attentive service shape an experience that feels both elevated and effortless.

Come for a restorative weekend, a long-awaited family holiday, or a celebration worth remembering. From a slow breakfast to the last light over the water, our aim is to make each part of your stay feel personal.

Dining at Azure

Flavours worth lingering over.

Good food has a way of bringing people together. Our dining concept celebrates seasonal ingredients, thoughtful preparation, and the pleasure of taking your time. Begin with a relaxed breakfast, return for a leisurely lunch, or settle in for a memorable dinner.

Azure Restaurant offers contemporary coastal cuisine in an elegant setting. The Palm Terrace is imagined for open-air dining and unhurried mornings, while The Azure Lounge provides a comfortable spot for light bites and evening conversation.

Guests with dietary preferences or food allergies are encouraged to share their requirements with the team when making an enquiry, so suitable options can be discussed in advance.

Experiences

Let the day take its own shape.

Some days are made for exploring; others are best spent doing very little. At The Grand Azure, the experience is about having the freedom to choose. Take a refreshing swim, find a sunny corner with a book, or enjoy a quiet walk along the shore.

The resort experience is imagined around a tranquil infinity pool, relaxed beach time, and thoughtful opportunities to reconnect with the outdoors. Our team can help guests explore local activities and plan the details of a day that feels right for them.
