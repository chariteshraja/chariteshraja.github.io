ABOUT OUR CLINIC

A thoughtful approach to dental care

At BrightSmile Dental, the fictional clinic featured in this portfolio project, the focus is on making dental information approachable and easy to understand.

The website demonstrates how a clinic can explain its services, answer common questions, and help visitors find the next step.

  • Clear and accessible service information
  • Simple appointment enquiry process
  • Responsive design for mobile visitors
  • Easy-to-navigate website structure
